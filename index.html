About Me

I'm Zhiwei Yang, a Ph.D. student at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS), advised by Prof. Lei Jiang.

My research focuses on natural language processing and data mining, with particular interests in:

  • AI agents, post-training of LLMs/VLMs, and multimodal large language models
  • Multimodal social event detection, multimodal content mining, and structural entropy

Contact: yangzhiwei@iie.ac.cn · yzw377986686@gmail.com
