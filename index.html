About Me

Hi! I'm Zhiwei Yang (杨志伟), at the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS). My research focuses on multimodal intelligence and trustworthy AI.

My research interests include:

  • Multimodal Social Event Detection (Unsupervised Learning, Open-world Learning, and Event Understanding)
  • Structural Entropy and Graph Learning (Graph Representation, Clustering, and Incremental Learning)
  • Trustworthy AI (Privacy-preserving Learning and AI Agent Safety)

Email: yangzhiwei@iie.ac.cn
